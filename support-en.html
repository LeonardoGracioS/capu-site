Support

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to samuelgracio@gmail.com, I usually answer within 48 hours.

Add the widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap “Edit” then “Add Widget”, search for “Capu” and pick a size.

The weather isn't my city's

In Capu, tap the city name at the top: choose “My location” or a city. The widget follows your location if you allow it (Settings > Capu > Location).

Restore my purchases

Capu > “…” > Settings > Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account you bought with.

My data

Everything stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases all its data. See the privacy policy.
