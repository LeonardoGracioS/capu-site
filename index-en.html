Your weather monkey

Capu is a little capuchin monkey who lives on your Home Screen. Every morning he dresses for your city's weather and tells you, in one sentence, whether you need a jacket, an umbrella or nothing at all.

  • Small, medium and Lock Screen widgets.
  • Rain to the hour, feels-like, tomorrow and 10 days.
  • An album of rare days: first snow, heatwave, storm…
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
